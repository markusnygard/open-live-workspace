Recorder UI — Floating Bar (Lower Right)

When the production has recorder outputs assigned, an orange Recorder icon appears in the top bar. Clicking it toggles this floating bar in the lower right corner. Each recorder shows its name, source, format, status, and controls. Duration counter only appears when actively recording.
